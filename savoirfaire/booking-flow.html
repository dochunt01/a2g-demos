Weddings vs. everything else

Weddings are priced by parts and spaces, never hours — ceremony, cocktail hour, and reception in one room is the base price. Other events are priced in coverage tiers: up to 2 hours, 3 hours, 4 hours, then a conversation.

These don’t contradict each other. A wedding in one room is about two hours of actual playing spread across a long afternoon — the same music as the two-hour corporate booking, described in each market’s own language.

What the form deliberately does not do

It never makes the customer price their own guest count, and it never explains why a violin dislikes rain. Both of those are Sam’s job on the phone, or the agreement’s job in writing. The form’s only job is to get to a number and a name.
