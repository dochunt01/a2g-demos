🎯 What the reels show

  • You talking to camera (“I refuse to explain my value…”) — best of the week: 275 plays, 5 comments. People want you, not just the violin.
  • Full tunes, labeled (Cherokee, Elegy, Busta) — the deepest engagement: 10.4% and 8.1%.
  • Story / throwback (DuSable 2019) — 10.3% but the TikTok watermark buries it on Instagram.
  • Filler (photo montages, quote cards) — 45–66 plays. That's the floor; it drags the average down.
  • Across everything: on-screen text is a title, not a hook · captions are hashtags only · no call-to-action anywhere.

🎬 The seven moves

1The sandwich. 5–10 seconds of you talking (a hook: “This tune got me thrown out of a lesson…”) → the performance → one line at the end: “book a date” / “tickets.”

2Live in 30–90 seconds. Keep the long full tunes as anchors — with a hook up front. Move the 14-second filler to Stories.

3Text on screen = hook, not title. “A violin isn't supposed to do this to a Busta Rhymes beat” beats the song name. Caption: hook first, hashtags last.

4Alternate the two lanes. Crossover (Busta, MJ) brings new fans; standards (Cherokee, Elegy) feed the people who already love you. Program them like a set list.

5Weekly throwback. The archive works (DuSable proved it) — just re-export without the TikTok watermark.

615 minutes a day in the comments. Reply to every single one — it's the cheapest growth there is. Pin your 3 best reels (Cherokee, the monologue, DuSable).

7One name, one link. Once the Artist Page is up: same handle on Instagram and Facebook, post natively to both (no watermarks), and every reel points at the same link-in-bio → the booking form, the day it's live.
